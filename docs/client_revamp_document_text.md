18. FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
What types of events does Iragu Events manage?
We manage weddings, birthdays, baptisms, anniversaries, corporate events, institutional functions, exhibitions, brand activations, public events and large-scale celebrations.
Do you provide customised décor?
Yes. Customisation is one of our key strengths. We design décor based on your expectations, theme, venue, preferences and budget.
Do you provide complete event planning?
Yes. We can manage your event from planning and vendor coordination through to event-day execution.
Can Iragu coordinate multiple vendors?
Absolutely. We can coordinate décor, catering, photography, videography, sound, lighting, entertainment and other event requirements.
What is the starting price for decoration?
Our customised décor starts from ₹20,000. The final price depends on the venue, theme, décor elements, setup size and customisation.

Yes. These can be included as part of your complete event requirements.
How early should I book my event?
We recommend contacting us as early as possible, especially for weddings and large-scale events. However, we also handle short-lead-time events depending on availability.
Do you travel outside Nagercoil?
Yes. We take up events across South Tamil Nadu and beyond, depending on the project requirements.
